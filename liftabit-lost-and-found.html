Tips for a faster match

  • Be specific — "navy blue backpack with a keychain" beats "a bag"
  • Include the exact area, not just the building
  • Add a photo of a similar item if you have one
  • Check Browse Listings first — it may already be found

Before you hand it in

  • Keep the item somewhere safe until claimed
  • Avoid sharing sensitive contents publicly (cards, IDs)
  • Liftabit staff may reach out to verify ownership
  • Unclaimed items are held for 30 days
